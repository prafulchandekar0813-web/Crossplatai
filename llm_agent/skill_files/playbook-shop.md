name: playbook-shop
description: Playbook for online shops and catalogues (fashion, gifts, electronics, handmade): screens, navigation, palette options, signature features, manage.json sections, sources.
---
Use this together with app-design. Study and adapt it; never copy a real brand.

WHO IT IS FOR: shoppers who browse by look, compare, and decide on a product page; owners who want to keep stock and prices current themselves.

SCREENS (Android, bottom tabs): Home, Shop (all products with filters), Wishlist, Cart, Account or Help.
- Home: a full-width hero with a strong picture and one button, featured collections as large image tiles, "New arrivals" and "Best sellers" rows, a trust strip (free returns, secure payment, delivery time), reviews with star ratings, an email or WhatsApp sign-up.
- Shop: a filter and sort bar (category, price, size), grid of 2 columns on phones, 4 on wide screens, sale badges and "only 2 left" labels.
- Product: picture gallery with swipe, price, variants (size, colour chips), delivery estimate, accordion for details and returns, a fixed "Add to cart" bar, and "You may also like".
- Cart and checkout: items, coupon field, address and payment summary, order confirmation screen.
WINDOWS: a top header with search and a mega-menu or side category list, filters in a left column, product grid on the right, product page with the gallery on the left and details on the right.

PALETTE OPTIONS: warm neutral #1c1917 text on #faf7f2 with a terracotta #c2410c accent; or clean white with an ink-blue #1e3a8a accent and a soft grey #f1f5f9 surface. Serif headlines with a clean sans body make a premium feel.

SIGNATURE FEATURES: wishlist hearts, size guide, colour chips, review stars with counts, low-stock badge, recently viewed, a clear return policy line.

manage.json SECTIONS: products (name, price, sale price, category, sizes, colours, stock, image, description), collections (title, image), reviews (name, rating, text), orders (inbox). 12 to 20 starter products.

SOURCES TO READ with read_url: Material Design 3 (m3.material.io) for cards, chips, navigation; Baymard Institute's product page and checkout research (baymard.com/blog); Fluent 2 (fluent2.microsoft.design) for the Windows layout; GitHub open-source storefront templates (MIT or Apache) for structure.
